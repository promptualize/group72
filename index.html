The
Seventy‑Two.

Luke 10 describes how Jesus sent seventy-two ordinary people out ahead of him, in pairs, saying: “The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few.”

Beyond our call as believers to find the lost, this line also rings true in the insurance industry. Far too many “agents” but not enough true workers tending a harvest on your behalf. This trend has resulted in a level of mediocrity in the industry.

Renewals show up at the last minute. Calls and emails go days without a response. Audits end in surprise bills, claims get denied, and your business is treated like just another policy on the books. If that sounds familiar, you are not alone. Group 72 was founded to change that, by bringing back the kind of service every client deserves.

Founder and President

Seven years in commercial property and casualty insurance, with a heavy focus on construction, manufacturing and complex risk placement. Group 72 blends the best of both to deliver a balanced approach to price, coverage and service.
